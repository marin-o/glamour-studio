# Media Replacement Plan

Current files in this folder are temporary placeholders. Replace them only with
owner-approved salon media from the real studio.

## Target Slots

- `salon-hero-video.mp4`: optional scroll or hero video, ideally hair movement
  filmed in the real studio.
- `salon-hero-poster.jpg`: required static fallback for the hero video.
- `salon-hair-texture.jpg`: close detail of hair movement, finish, or styling.
- `salon-interior-detail.jpg`: real salon interior, materials, or warm light.
- `salon-service-detail.jpg`: tools, color, prep, or service moment.
- `salon-signature-wide.jpg`: wide lookbook image for a strong visual moment.
- `salon-staff-or-owner.jpg`: optional staff or owner image, only with approval.

## Rules

- Do not use generic stock imagery as final salon media.
- Do not publish photos with people unless the owner confirms permission.
- Keep video and images lightweight enough for mobile loading.
- Keep placeholder filenames until final assets arrive.
